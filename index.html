Safety First, Every Day
Daily Safety Checklist

Four checks for a safer kitchen

Quick preventive habits that every LPG household should remember.

  • 01
    Keep It Upright

    Place the cylinder vertically on a stable, level surface.

  • 02
    Inspect Connections

    Check the regulator and hose condition at regular intervals.

  • 03
    Allow Ventilation

    Keep the cooking area airy and never block ventilation points.

  • 04
    Respond Correctly

    Turn off the regulator and call authorized support if leakage is suspected.
